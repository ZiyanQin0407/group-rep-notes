# AI Usage Disclosure

This document clarifies the use of generative AI in these notes. Transparency is essential in mathematics, and I want readers to know exactly what they are reading.

## Human-Written Content
- **Main Text (Chapters 1–12)**: Written and verified by the author.
- **Appendix A (Module Theory Preliminaries)** and **Appendix B (Algebraic Number Theory Preliminaries)**: Initial drafts were generated with the assistance of an AI tool.

## AI-Generated Drafts (Appendix A and B)
Because my current knowledge of algebraic number theory and advanced module theory is still developing, I used AI to draft these appendices. 

**However, I do not trust AI blindly.** I am currently in the process of verifying every definition, theorem, and proof in Appendix A and B against standard textbooks (e.g., Atiyah-MacDonald, Neukirch, Serre, Milne). 

## How You Can Help
Because AI can hallucinate, there may be subtle mathematical errors, missing hypotheses, or incorrect references in the appendices. If you find any mistake, please open an Issue or submit a Pull Request. 

I take full responsibility for any remaining errors. Your corrections will be greatly appreciated and acknowledged.
